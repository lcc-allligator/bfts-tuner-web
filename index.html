Offsets (cents)
Open strings: 6th E −2, 5th A −2, 4th D −2, 3rd G −2, 2nd B +1, 1st E 0. Drop D (6th string): −4. 12th fret: E 0, B 0, G +1, D +1, A 0, E 0. Use the Buzz Feiten presets only on a guitar with a Buzz Feiten nut; on any other guitar use Standard.
Tip: hold the phone near the soundhole or pickup and pluck one string. Green means within 1 cent. Attack mode reads the first moments of each pluck and holds that reading, so you tune to the attack, not to where the note settles (notes settle flat). Re-strike to update it. The tone button plays the selected string, including its offset, so you can tune by ear; detection pauses while it plays.
